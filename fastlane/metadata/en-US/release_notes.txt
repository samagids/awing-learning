What's new in 1.19.3:

• New Awing keyboard: when you type in a text field that needs Awing
  (Contribute, Translate, Report a wrong translation), the app now
  shows an in-app keyboard with all the Awing letters (ɛ ɔ ə ɨ ŋ),
  the curly glottal stop, and tone marks (á à â ǎ). No more fighting
  with the phone's default keyboard.

• On-device AI: when you turn off Cloud AI in settings, example
  sentences are now generated locally by a small language model on
  your device. Correct label and colour show which AI is being used.
  No internet needed once the model is downloaded.

• Fresh-download reliability: deleting the offline AI model in
  Settings now actually removes the file from disk. The re-download
  after that fetches a clean copy instead of trusting the previous
  cache.

• Full-screen support for newer Android versions (edge-to-edge).

• Assorted bug fixes and voice tuning.

Built with love for the Awing community.
