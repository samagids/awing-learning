📖 Read this first — how to use this page __PERSONAL_WELCOME__

1) Enable the microphone

This page works offline. The browser must be allowed to use your microphone.

  • Best: Open this file in Firefox (free download: firefox.com). When you start recording the first time, Firefox will ask permission — click Allow.
  • If using Chrome / Edge: microphones do not work on local files. Ask the person who sent you this file to give you the localhost version, OR install Firefox.

2) Type your name at the top

Your name gets added to every recording filename so we know who contributed.

3) Record each word

  1. Click the green 🎤 Record button next to a word.
  2. Speak clearly, naturally, the way you would say the word to a child.
  3. Click ⏹ Stop when done.
  4. Your browser will save the recording to your Downloads folder automatically (filename like native_marie__apo.webm).
  5. Click ▶ Play to listen. If unhappy, click 🔁 Re-record to do it again.

4) When you are done — send everything back

  1. Click the green Download all as ZIP button at the bottom of this page.
  2. You'll get one ZIP file containing every recording you made.
  3. Email or WhatsApp that ZIP to Dr. Guidion Sama at samagids@gmail.com.

Tips

  • You don't have to record everything in one sitting. Your progress is saved automatically in this browser.
  • Quiet room is best. Speak about 6 inches from the microphone.
  • Use the Beginner words filter first — those are the most common words children will learn.
  • If you don't know how to say a word, skip it. Just leave it un-recorded.
