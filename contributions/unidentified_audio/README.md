# Unidentified native recordings

Eleven clips whose key is shared by several Awing words, and which
Dr. Sama listened to on the Awing Clip Binding page without being able to
say which word was recorded.

They are NOT deleted. These are irreplaceable native recordings and the
audio is kept here in case the word is recognised later. What was removed
is their place in the app: the files are out of
android/install_time_assets/.../audio/native/vocabulary/, so no card plays
them, and every word that shared the key now shows as needing a native
voice in Contribute -> Record.

To restore one, move the .opus and .wav back and re-run
scripts/build_native_audio_manifest.py.
