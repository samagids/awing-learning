🎙️ Awing Family Voice Recording Studio

How this works (read me first)
  1. Each Awing item below shows its meaning in English plus six record buttons -- one per family member.
  2. Click your name's mic to start recording. Click again to stop.
  3. When you stop, two things happen:
    • Your browser downloads the recording as boy_joel__apo.webm (or similar).
    • A copy stays inside this page so you can press ▶ Play to listen to it before deciding whether to re-record.
  4. Move all downloaded files from your Downloads folder into the family_recordings/ folder of the project.
  5. This page remembers who recorded what AND keeps the audio (stored locally in your browser's database) until you press Reset progress.
  6. If recording does not start: you opened this file by double-clicking. Microphones need localhost. Run scripts\serve_family_recorder.bat instead.
